# Kevin Cookie Company intro

A six-second Remotion animation: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps (180 frames).
All cookie illustrations are original inline SVG; no remote assets or fonts are required.

## Preview

```sh
npm ci
npm start
```

Open the Studio address printed in your terminal and select `KevinCookieCompany`.
Press Space to play; scrub the timeline to inspect individual frames.

## Check

```sh
npm run typecheck
```

The giant cookie drops and lands at 0.6 seconds, with a squash-and-stretch
bounce, a 0.35-second damped screen shake, and flying crumbs. At 1.5 seconds
it begins shrinking into the lower-right accent as the company name appears.
The tagline follows at 3.1 seconds; the final card holds through 6 seconds.
Colors and timing are in `src/Root.tsx`.
